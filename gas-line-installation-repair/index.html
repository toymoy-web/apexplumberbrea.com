Gas line work is safety-critical and requires a licensed plumber. We install new gas line runs for appliances and repair or re-route existing lines, all to California code. We also verify appliance connections meet current code even when only part of the system is being touched, since an inspection can flag unrelated issues if they're not addressed at the same time.

Why This Happens In Brea.

Local Cause.

Older Brea homes near the historic downtown core sometimes still have original black iron gas piping that predates current code requirements. That matters if you're adding an appliance like a tankless water heater or outdoor kitchen that needs more capacity than the original line was sized for.

Olinda Village's larger, older lots sometimes have gas lines running longer distances from the meter to outbuildings or detached structures, which affects sizing calculations differently than a standard in-house line extension.

Signs To Watch For.

Symptoms.

  • Smell of gas (rotten egg odor).
  • Hissing sound near a gas appliance or line.
  • Dead vegetation over a buried gas line.
  • Pilot lights that won't stay lit across multiple appliances.

How we diagnose it: For a suspected leak, we use a gas leak detector to locate the exact point before any repair. For new installs, we size the line to the appliance's BTU demand and pressure-test the completed run before it's put into service.

Options.

Repair Approaches.

🔧

Leak repair.

Locating and sealing or replacing the failed section of an existing line.

New line installation.

What To Expect.

On The Visit.

For a suspected leak, we trace the exact location with a calibrated gas detector before opening anything, since gas line work is safety-critical and precision matters. For new installation, we size the line to the appliance's BTU demand, coordinate any required permit and inspection, and pressure-test the completed run before it's put into service.

Timing.

Emergency Call or Schedule It?.

Any suspected gas leak (rotten-egg smell, hissing near a line) is always an emergency, leave the house and call immediately. New line installation for a planned appliance addition is a scheduled project with no urgency. Either way, call (714) 750-8637 and we'll tell you honestly which one applies.

Before We Arrive.

What To Do While You Wait.

  • If you smell gas (a rotten-egg odor), leave the house immediately, don't operate light switches or any electrical device.
  • Once outside, if you can safely reach the gas meter, shut off the gas supply there.
  • Call us or your gas utility from outside the house, not from inside.
  • Don't attempt to locate or repair a suspected gas leak yourself, this is licensed, safety-critical work.
Answered First.

FAQs.

What should I do if I smell gas?
Leave the house immediately, don't operate any switches or appliances, and call from outside. If you can safely shut off the gas at the meter, do so, then call us.
Do I need a permit for new gas line work?
Most gas line installation and significant repair work in Brea requires a permit and inspection. We handle that as part of the job.
How do you test a gas line for leaks?
We use a calibrated gas leak detector to pinpoint the exact location, and pressure-test any new or repaired line before it's put into service.

Gas line issues aren't a DIY or wait-and-see situation. If you smell gas or suspect a leak, treat it as an emergency and call immediately.
