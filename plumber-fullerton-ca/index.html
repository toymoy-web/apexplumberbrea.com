A Bigger City With Older Bones.

Fullerton sits just southwest of Brea, and northern Fullerton shares a border with our home city, so it is one of the shortest drives we make. The center of town, around the historic train depot and Downtown Fullerton, is roughly five miles away.

Fullerton is much older than most of its neighbors. It incorporated in 1904 and was built out in layers, so one service call can involve very different plumbing depending on the street. A 1920s bungalow near downtown, a 1950s ranch in Sunny Hills, and a condo near Cal State Fullerton each fail in different ways, so we ask about the age of the home before we ask about the symptom.

Local Housing.

Housing From The 1910s Through The 1970s.

The blocks around downtown and Hillcrest Park have a large stock of early-1900s through 1930s bungalows and Craftsman homes. Many still have original clay or cast-iron drain lines and galvanized supply pipe unless a past owner replaced them. North Fullerton, including Sunny Hills along the Brea border, was built mostly in the 1950s through 1970s on hillside tract lots. West Fullerton, including Amerige Heights, is newer construction with modern copper or PEX lines. Near Cal State Fullerton and along the main corridors there are also apartments and condos, where one failed shutoff or water heater can affect several units.

What this means for pipe age: pre-1940 homes near downtown often have original cast-iron or clay drains and galvanized supply lines, while 1950s to 1970s homes in north Fullerton may have early copper that is nearing the end of its service life.

Before We Arrive.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Fullerton.

Older homes near downtown can hide their shutoffs in unexpected places. A few steps while you wait on us:

📡

Shut off the water.

In older central Fullerton homes the main shutoff is often at the street meter box or on the side of the house instead of in the garage, so find it before you need it.

⚡

Kill power near the water.

If water is near an outlet, panel, or appliance, switch off that circuit at the breaker when you can reach it safely.

🛡

Protect floors and belongings.

Pull rugs, boxes, and electronics off wet floors, and in a multi-unit building let the neighbor below know right away.
