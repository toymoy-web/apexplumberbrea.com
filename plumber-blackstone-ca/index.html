If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Blackstone.

Blackstone's hillside grading means a slab leak can show up differently than a standard pipe leak. Either way, here's what to do first:

📡

Shut off the water.

Blackstone's newer homes typically have an easy-to-find shutoff in the garage or a utility closet, know its location before an emergency, not during one.

⚡

Watch for slab-related warning signs.

A warm spot on the floor or new cracking in flooring can point to a slab leak, note where you see it so we can get there fast.

🛡

Clear the immediate area.

Move furniture and electronics away from any standing water, hillside homes can have finished lower levels that are easy to overlook.
