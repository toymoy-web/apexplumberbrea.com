Why This Happens In Brea.

Local Cause.

Brea's older housing stock near Downtown Brea and the 1970s Country Road townhomes commonly still run original or first-replacement water heaters well past their 8-12 year expected service life. If your unit hasn't been replaced since the home was built or last sold, sediment buildup from mineral-rich water can already be reducing efficiency and shortening the tank's remaining life, which is worth factoring into a repair-versus-replace decision.

In Blackstone's newer construction, water heaters are typically original to the 2000s build and haven't hit the failure window yet. A repair call there is more often a single component issue than a tank nearing end of life, worth knowing before assuming the worst.

Signs To Watch For.

Symptoms.

  • No hot water or lukewarm water only.
  • Rust-colored water from the hot tap.
  • Popping or rumbling noises from the tank.
  • Water pooling at the base of the unit.
  • Pilot light won't stay lit (gas units).

How we diagnose it: We test the thermostat, heating element (electric) or gas valve and thermocouple (gas), check for tank corrosion, and flush for sediment where that's the actual issue rather than a symptom of a failing tank.

Tank, Fittings Or Relief Valve.

Where A Water Heater Leak Is Really Coming From.

A leak is the single most common reason people call us about a water heater. Some callers see a puddle at the base of the tank, some see water dripping from a connection, and some find the leak in an attic closet through a stained ceiling below. Where the water is coming from decides whether the heater can be saved.

Leaks at fittings, the drain valve, or the temperature and pressure relief valve are often repairable. Water seeping from the tank itself usually means the tank has failed and the unit needs replacing. We tell you which one it is before any work starts, and we say so plainly when a repair would not be worth the money.

Timing.

Water Heater Leak: Emergency Or Can It Wait?.

Call right away if you have no hot water at all, or if you see water actively pooling at the base of the tank, that's a sign the tank itself may be failing and could leak further at any time.

FAQs.

Is it worth repairing an old water heater instead of replacing it?
If the tank itself isn't leaking or corroded and the unit is under about 8 years old, a repair usually makes sense. Past that, a repair is often a short-term fix on a unit that's going to fail again soon.
Why does my water heater smell like rotten eggs?
That's typically a reaction between the anode rod and sulfur bacteria in the water supply, not a gas leak. We can replace the anode rod, though a persistent smell on an older tank is often another sign it's near the end of its life.
Can you repair a water heater the same day you're called?
For most component-level repairs, yes. If the tank itself needs replacing, we'll usually have a new unit installed the same or next day depending on availability.
Do you work on both gas and electric water heaters?
Yes, we service and repair both gas and electric tank water heaters, using the appropriate diagnostic process for each.
Do you repair water heaters in Brea at night and on weekends?
Yes. Our emergency line is answered 24/7, including nights, weekends, and holidays. A tank that is leaking, a heater flooding a garage, or a ceiling dripping from an attic unit all count as emergencies. If you smell gas, leave the home and call SoCalGas or 911 first, then call us.
My water heater is in the attic. Does a leak there need urgent attention?
Yes. Water in an attic closet soaks insulation and drywall quickly and can come through the ceiling below. Close the cold supply valve on the heater, turn off the power or gas, and call our 24/7 line.
