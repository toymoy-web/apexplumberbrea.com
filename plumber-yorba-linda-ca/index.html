Local Housing.

From 1970s Custom Homes To Newer Master-Planned Streets.

Much of Yorba Linda was built from the 1970s onward, with custom and semi-custom homes on large lots, followed by newer master-planned neighborhoods. Homes at the older end of that range are now 40 to 50 years old, which is when original copper can develop pinhole leaks and water heaters and pressure regulators wear out. On sloped lots, a failing pressure regulator can push pressure high enough to stress fixtures and supply lines.

What this means for pipe age: 1970s and 1980s custom homes often have original copper that can develop pinhole leaks, and hillside lots usually depend on a pressure regulator that wears out with age.

Before We Arrive.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Yorba Linda.

Large lots and sloped streets mean longer walks and higher pressure. A few steps while you wait on us:

📡

Find the main shutoff first.

On a large lot the main shutoff may sit near the street or at the side of the property, not by the house. Head there while you are on the phone with us.

⚡

Protect pool and yard equipment.

If the leak is in the yard, turn off power to the pool pump or irrigation controller before water reaches the equipment pad.

🛡

Watch the pressure.

A hose that sprays hard or fixtures that bang when closed point to high pressure from a failing regulator, which is worth telling us about when you call.

On The Visit In Yorba Linda.

On a Yorba Linda call we ask about the age of the home, whether you have a pressure regulator, and how far the main shutoff sits from the house. We measure static pressure at an outdoor spigot, then check the water heater and visible copper lines for corrosion. Emergency Call or Schedule It?.

A burst line, a leak you cannot isolate, or sewage backing into a drain justifies an emergency call. Pressure that has crept up over months, or a water heater that is old but working, can wait for a scheduled visit. Either way, call (714) 750-8637 and we'll tell you honestly which one applies.

FAQs From Yorba Linda.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Yorba Linda?
Yes. We cover 92886 and 92887, which together include the central, western, and eastern parts of the city, and we also take calls tied to the 92885 post office box code. The adjoining Brea hillside ZIP 92823 is covered too.
Can you test and replace a failing pressure regulator?
Yes. High water pressure on a hillside lot wears out fixtures and supply lines, so we measure pressure at an outdoor spigot and replace the regulator if it has failed.
Can you service pool plumbing and outdoor lines on a large Yorba Linda lot?
Yes. Larger properties have longer outdoor runs, hose bibs, and pool equipment lines, and we trace the full run instead of only the fixtures closest to the house.
Is emergency service available in Yorba Linda?
Yes. Our emergency line is answered 24/7 for Yorba Linda, including nights, weekends, and holidays. On a large lot the main shutoff can be a long walk from the house, so call us while you head there.
Do you serve the Vista del Verde community?
Yes. Vista del Verde is a master-planned community in northern Yorba Linda, built around Black Gold Golf Club, and we take calls there like anywhere else in the city. Newer homes there usually raise different questions than the older custom homes, such as water heater sizing and softener setup.
