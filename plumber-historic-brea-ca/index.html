Historic Residential Streets.

Plumbing Service For Brea's Historic Residential Streets.

Historic Brea is the label the City of Brea's 2003 General Plan uses for older neighborhoods in southwest Brea, centered on South Walnut, South Madrona and South Redwood Avenues near Brea City Hall and Park. The plan describes the South Walnut neighborhood around the park and notes that many of its structures were 80 to 90 years old at that time.

City Hall and Park, at 401 South Brea Boulevard, opened in 1928 and was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1984. A neighborhood that Union Oil developed in the 1920s for oil field workers and their families sits west of the park. We describe this area by its anchor streets because we have not found an official boundary.

Local Housing.

Early Twentieth Century Homes Near City Hall Park.

The Brea Museum's Historic Resources Register, last updated in February 2022, lists 58 local resources. Most are individual houses, and the largest groups are on South Madrona, South Walnut and South Redwood Avenues. A home of this age may or may not have had its plumbing updated over the years, and we cannot tell from the street.

What this means for pipe age: We confirm what is actually installed in your home with an inspection instead of assuming it from the street or the year the house was built.

Before We Arrive.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Historic Brea.

A leak in an older home can spread before it shows. While you wait for us:

💧

Shut off the water.

Find your main shutoff. Depending on the house it can be inside, in the garage, along a side yard or at the street meter. Turn any older valve gently and do not force it.

⚡

Protect floors and belongings.

Move rugs, furniture and electronics away from standing water and soak up what you can, since water can damage wood floors and trim quickly.

📷

Note where the water appears.

A photo of the wet area and a note of when you first noticed it help us find the source faster.

FAQs From Historic Brea.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Historic Brea?
The historic streets around City Hall Park are in ZIP 92821, and we cover all of it. If you are unsure which ZIP your address uses, call and we will confirm.
Is emergency service available in Historic Brea?
Yes. Our emergency line runs 24/7 for Historic Brea, including older homes where the shutoff is hard to find.
Is Historic Brea an official city district?
We have not found an official boundary for it. The City's General Plan uses Historic Brea as a label for older neighborhoods in southwest Brea, and the Brea Museum keeps a register of historic resources. We describe the area by its anchor streets.
Does an older home need different plumbing work?
Not automatically. Age alone does not tell us what is installed, so we inspect first and recommend a repair or replacement only after we see the actual condition.
Do I need approval to repair plumbing in a registered historic home?
We cannot answer that for your property, because it depends on the property. Ask the City of Brea Planning Division before you plan visible changes. For an active leak, call us right away.
