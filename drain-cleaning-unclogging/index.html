A slow or fully clogged drain is one of the most common calls we take in Brea, and most jobs are resolved the same visit with cabling or snaking. We clear kitchen, bathroom, and main line clogs without defaulting to hydro jetting when a simpler method will do the job.

Why This Happens In Brea.

Local Cause.

Older cast-iron drain lines near Brea's historic downtown core are more prone to internal roughness that catches debris and forms recurring clogs at the same spots, which is worth knowing if you're dealing with a drain that clears temporarily and clogs again within weeks.

In Olinda Village, where lots are larger and often on septic-to-sewer transition history, a slow drain complaint sometimes traces back further into the property's own lateral line rather than the city main, which changes where we start looking.

Signs To Watch For.

Symptoms.

  • Water draining slowly.
  • Gurgling sounds from drains or toilets.
  • Multiple fixtures backing up at once.
  • Recurring clogs at the same drain.

How we diagnose it: We use a drain snake or motorized cable to physically break up and retrieve the clog. For a drain that clogs repeatedly, we run a camera inspection to find out whether it's a one-time blockage or a structural issue like a bellied or root-intruded line.

Options.

Repair Approaches.

🔧

Cabling/snaking.

Standard first approach for most single-fixture clogs and slow drains.

🔧

Hydro jetting.

Needed for grease buildup or recurring clogs cabling doesn't fully clear; see our Hydro Jetting page.

🔧

Camera inspection.

Recommended when the same drain clogs repeatedly, to rule out a structural cause before repeat service calls.

What To Expect.

On The Visit.

We ask which drains are affected and whether it's happened before, since a single slow sink and multiple backed-up fixtures point to different causes. We access the clog through the nearest cleanout where possible to avoid pulling a fixture, and run water through the line afterward to confirm it's actually clear, not just temporarily improved.

Timing.

Emergency Call or Schedule It?.

Multiple drains backing up at once, or water backing up from a lower drain when you run an upper fixture, points to a main line issue and is worth an emergency call. A single slow sink or shower drain can usually wait for a scheduled appointment. Either way, call (714) 750-8637 and we'll tell you honestly which one applies.

FAQs.

Why does my drain clog again a few weeks after cleaning?
That usually means cabling cleared the immediate blockage but not the underlying cause, like grease buildup along the pipe wall or a structural issue further down the line. A camera inspection tells us which.
Is hydro jetting better than snaking?
Not universally. Snaking is faster and less expensive for a simple clog. Hydro jetting clears the full pipe wall and is the better call for grease buildup or a history of recurring clogs.
Can a clogged drain be an emergency?
Yes, especially if multiple fixtures are backing up at once, which usually points to a main line issue rather than a single clogged fixture. That's worth an emergency call rather than waiting.
What should I avoid putting down my drains to prevent clogs?
Grease and cooking oil are the biggest culprits, they solidify inside the pipe as they cool. Coffee grounds, eggshells, and fibrous vegetable scraps are also common causes of kitchen drain clogs.

One slow drain is usually a quick fix. Multiple drains backing up together is a different problem, call us right away rather than treating each fixture separately. For a clog that keeps coming back after cabling, our hydro jetting service clears the full pipe wall instead. If the issue turns out to be a hidden supply-line leak rather than a drain clog, see leak detection. This is one of the most common calls we take from Downtown Brea.
