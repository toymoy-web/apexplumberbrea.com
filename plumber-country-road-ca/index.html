Before We Arrive.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Country Road.

In a shared-wall townhome, water travels fast to the unit next door. Here's what to do right away:

📡

Shut off the water.

Check your unit's own shutoff first, usually near the water heater closet, most Country Road leaks are contained to one unit's fixtures.

⚡

Kill power to wet areas.

If water is near an outlet or panel, shut off power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

🛡

Check for a shared-wall impact.

In a townhome, water can travel to the unit next door faster than you'd expect, let a neighbor know if there's any chance it's affected them too.
