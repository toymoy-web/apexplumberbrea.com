A slab leak, a leak in the water line running under your home's concrete foundation, is a distinct diagnostic and repair challenge from a standard leak. We use specialized equipment to locate slab leaks precisely and offer repair options that don't always require breaking through the foundation.

Why This Happens In Brea.

Local Cause.

Brea's newer hillside developments like Blackstone, built on graded lots, see slab leaks driven more by foundation settling than by pipe age. Older flatland homes near Downtown Brea are more likely to see slab leaks from original copper or galvanized lines corroding after decades under the slab, and that difference changes what repair approach makes sense.

In Downtown Brea's older flatland homes, original copper lines under the slab are now decades past typical service life, making age-related slab leaks more common there than in Blackstone's newer, differently-caused settling-related leaks.

Signs To Watch For.

Symptoms.

  • Warm spot on the floor with no explanation.
  • Sound of running water when everything is off.
  • Unexplained spike in water bill.
  • Cracks appearing in flooring or the foundation itself.

How we diagnose it: We use acoustic and electronic detection specifically calibrated for under-slab leaks to pinpoint the location precisely, since guessing wrong on a slab job means breaking concrete in the wrong spot.

Options.

Repair Approaches.

🔧

Direct access repair.

Breaking through the slab at the precise leak location, appropriate for an isolated, accessible leak.

🔧

Reroute above the slab.

Running a new line through the attic or walls instead of under the slab, often less invasive for certain layouts.

Common Question.

How Do You Know It's a Slab Leak and Not a Regular Leak?.

A warm spot on the floor (hot water line), a spike in your water bill with no visible leak anywhere else, or cracking in the flooring or foundation are the clearest slab-specific signs. A leak behind a wall or under a sink is a different diagnostic path, covered on our general Leak Detection page instead.

What To Expect.

On The Visit.

We use acoustic and electronic detection specifically calibrated for under-slab leaks to narrow the location before recommending any access point, since guessing wrong means breaking concrete in the wrong spot. We'll present both direct-access repair and above-slab reroute options with real cost differences for your home's specific layout.

Timing.

Emergency Call or Schedule It?.

A warm floor spot with a rising water bill is worth investigating promptly, since a slab leak causes more structural damage the longer it runs undetected. If you've already confirmed a slab leak with no immediate flooring damage, scheduling within a few days for repair is reasonable. Before We Arrive.

What To Do While You Wait.

  • A warm spot on the floor, especially if it's new, is worth checking rather than ignoring, it usually means a hot water line is leaking under the slab.
  • Watch for any cracking in flooring, tile, or the foundation itself and note when it started.
  • Check your water meter's leak indicator with all fixtures off, if it's moving, a leak is present somewhere in the system.
  • Avoid drilling, nailing, or otherwise penetrating the slab yourself in the suspected area.

FAQs.

Do you have to break my foundation to fix a slab leak?
Not always. Depending on the home's layout, rerouting the line above the slab can sometimes avoid breaking concrete entirely. We'll present both options with real cost differences.
Is a slab leak covered by insurance?
Many policies cover the cost of accessing and repairing the leak itself, though coverage for the leaking pipe replacement varies by policy. Check with your insurer, we can document the repair for a claim.
Can a slab leak be prevented?
Not entirely, since it's driven by pipe age, water pressure, and soil movement, but catching it early through the warning signs above limits the damage and cost significantly compared to a leak that runs for months undetected.

Slab leaks get more expensive the longer they run, since they can undermine flooring and even foundation integrity. Don't wait on a warm floor spot or unexplained water bill increase. A leak that isn't under the slab, like one behind a wall or under a sink, is covered on our general leak detection page instead. If several sections of supply line are failing, see repiping and whole-home pipe replacement. This comes up most in Blackstone, where hillside grading is a factor.
