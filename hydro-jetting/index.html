Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water, not a cable, to scour the full interior of a drain or sewer line clean. It's the right call for grease buildup, root intrusion, and lines that keep re-clogging after standard cabling. For properties with a documented history of grease-related clogs, we can also set up a recurring jetting schedule to stay ahead of buildup rather than waiting for the next backup.

Why This Happens In Brea.

Local Cause.

Restaurants and older single-family kitchens in Brea's Downtown core with years of grease going down the drain are the most common candidates, along with homes near mature landscaping where tree roots have found their way into a clay or older sewer line.

Brea Hills properties with mature landscaping and larger yards are more likely to have root intrusion in the sewer lateral from established trees, which is the main reason we bring a root-cutting attachment as a standard option on hillside calls.

Signs To Watch For.

Symptoms.

  • Recurring clogs after cabling.
  • Multiple slow drains sitewide.
  • Sewer odor from drains.
  • Known root intrusion from a prior camera inspection.

How we diagnose it: A specialized nozzle feeds through the line and blasts water at high pressure in all directions, cutting through grease, scale, and root intrusion rather than just punching a channel through the blockage the way cabling does.

Options.

Repair Approaches.

Common Question.

How Do You Know If You Need Hydro Jetting Instead of Snaking?.

If a drain has been cabled before and clogs again within a few weeks to months, or if a camera inspection shows grease coating or root intrusion along the pipe wall rather than a single blockage, that's the signal jetting is the better fit. It clears the whole line instead of punching through one spot. We'll recommend it only when the evidence supports it, not as a default upsell.

What To Expect.

On The Visit.

Before jetting, we run a camera through the line to confirm what we're actually dealing with, grease buildup, root intrusion, or scale, since the nozzle and pressure settings differ by cause. After jetting, we run the camera again to confirm the line is genuinely clear along its full length, not just at the point we could see from the access point.

Timing.

Emergency Call or Schedule It?.

This is rarely an emergency service on its own, it's typically scheduled after a camera inspection confirms it's the right fix for a recurring or grease-related clog rather than a same-day reactive call. Either way, call (714) 750-8637 and we'll tell you honestly which one applies.

Answered First.

FAQs.

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?
It's safe for most cast iron and clay pipe in good structural condition. For a line we suspect is already deteriorated, we'll camera-inspect first rather than jet blind.
How often does a line need hydro jetting?
For a restaurant or a home with a history of grease-related clogs, annually is common. For a one-time root intrusion fix, it's not a recurring need unless roots grow back.
Will hydro jetting damage my pipes?
Used correctly on pipe in reasonable structural condition, no. We camera-inspect first on any line we're not confident about, since jetting a severely deteriorated pipe isn't the right approach for that pipe's condition.
