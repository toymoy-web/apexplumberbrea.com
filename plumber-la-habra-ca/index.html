Before We Arrive.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In La Habra.

Most La Habra homes are single-story on slabs, so water spreads across the floor quickly. A few steps while you wait on us:

📡

Shut off the water.

Look for the main shutoff valve near the front of the house or at the meter, and turn it fully clockwise. In a 1950s home the old valve can be stiff, so turn it slowly.

⚡

Turn off the water heater.

If the leak is near the water heater in the garage, switch off its power or set the gas control to pilot before water reaches the burner or the electrical connections.

🛡

Get water off the floor.

Slab homes spread water fast. Pull up rugs, lift boxes onto a counter, and push standing water toward a door or drain with a squeegee if you have one.
