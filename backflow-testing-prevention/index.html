Backflow prevention devices keep contaminated water from flowing back into the clean water supply, and many are required by code for irrigation systems and certain commercial connections. We test, certify, and install backflow prevention devices. We keep a record of your device's testing history, which makes future annual compliance faster since we already know the device model and its prior results.

Why This Happens In Brea.

Local Cause.

Properties in Brea with irrigation systems, particularly in the hillside developments and larger-lot neighborhoods like Olinda Village and Brea Hills, are the most likely to have a backflow device requiring annual testing under local water agency rules.

Brea Hills properties with elaborate multi-zone irrigation systems sometimes have more than one backflow device on the property, which affects both the testing time and the total cost.

Signs To Watch For.

Symptoms.

  • Annual testing notice from your water provider.
  • Discolored or unusual-tasting water.
  • New irrigation system installation requiring a device.
  • Failed test on a prior inspection.

How we diagnose it: We test the device under the required pressure conditions, document the results for submission to the water agency, and repair or replace the device on the spot if it fails.

Common Question.

Do You Actually Need Backflow Testing?.

If your property has an irrigation system or certain commercial water connections, your water provider likely requires annual testing and will send a notice. Residential homes without irrigation systems usually don't need this. If you're unsure, check for a backflow device near your irrigation valve or main shutoff, or ask us to check during a service call.

What To Expect.

On The Visit.

Testing takes place under the pressure conditions your water provider requires, and we document the results in the format needed for submission. If a device fails, we can often repair or replace it on the spot and resubmit passing results the same day, avoiding a return trip and a compliance gap with your provider.

Timing.

Emergency Call or Schedule It?.

Routine annual testing is never urgent and can be scheduled at your convenience within your provider's compliance window. A failed test that puts your water service at risk of restriction is worth prompt rescheduling. Either way, call (714) 750-8637 and we'll tell you honestly which one applies.

FAQs.

Is backflow testing required every year?
For most irrigation systems and certain commercial connections in this area, yes, annually. Your water provider typically sends a reminder notice.
What happens if my backflow device fails testing?
We can usually repair or replace it on the spot and resubmit passing results, avoiding a return trip.
What happens if I skip required backflow testing?
Your water provider can restrict or shut off service for non-compliance on properties where testing is required. It's worth staying current on the annual notice rather than letting it lapse.

If you've received a testing notice from your water provider, we can usually schedule and complete it within the same week. If you're not sure whether your property has a backflow device or needs testing, we can check during any other service visit at no extra trip.
