Local Housing.

Newer Hillside Homes On Terraced Lots.

Chino Hills incorporated in 1991, and most of its homes were built from the 1980s through the 2000s on rolling terrain. Many sit on terraced pads with retaining walls and drainage swales, which makes surface water and grading part of the plumbing picture alongside the pipes. Slab and raised foundations are both common, and tree roots are a recurring cause of drain and sewer trouble.

What this means for pipe age: 1980s to 2000s homes usually have copper or PEX supply lines, so the more common problems are aging water heaters, tree-root drain trouble, and drainage around terraced lots.

Before We Arrive.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Chino Hills.

Because we are coming over the canyon road, a few minutes of preparation before we arrive helps a lot:

📡

Shut off the water.

Close the main valve, usually near the front of the house or at the meter box, and open a low faucet to drain remaining pressure from the line.

⚡

Switch off power near water.

If water has reached a garage, lower level, or any outlet, switch off that circuit at the breaker if you can reach it safely.

🛡

Check the yard and slope.

If water is pooling on a terrace or against a retaining wall, note where it comes from, since it can be runoff and not a plumbing line.
