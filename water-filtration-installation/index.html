Whole-home and point-of-use water filtration systems address taste, odor, and specific contaminants beyond what a water softener handles. We install systems sized and specified for what's actually in your water. We walk you through the trade-offs between system types honestly rather than defaulting to the most expensive option, since a simple carbon filter solves taste and odor concerns that a full reverse osmosis system isn't needed for. Installation typically takes a few hours and doesn't require shutting off water to the whole house for long.

Why This Happens In Brea.

Local Cause.

Municipal water in Brea meets safety standards but can still carry chlorine taste and odor common to treated water systems, which is the most common reason Brea homeowners look into filtration even when water softness isn't the concern.

In Downtown Brea's older condo conversions, under-sink space can be tighter than in a single-family home, which affects which filtration system models actually fit the available cabinet space.

Signs To Watch For.

Symptoms.

  • Chlorine taste or smell from tap water.
  • preference for filtered water without buying bottled water.
  • specific contaminant concern from a water test.
  • existing filtration system needing replacement.
  • visible sediment in tap water.

How we diagnose it: We can test for specific concerns or install based on your priorities, whether that's a whole-home carbon filtration system for taste and odor or a point-of-use reverse osmosis system under the kitchen sink for drinking water. We also test your incoming water for the specific concern you've raised before recommending a system, rather than sizing based on general assumptions.

FAQs.

Do I need a water softener and a filtration system?
They solve different problems. A softener addresses hardness minerals; filtration addresses taste, odor, and specific contaminants. Some homes benefit from both, others just one.
How often do filtration system filters need replacing?
Depends on the system and water usage, typically every 6-12 months for most residential systems. We can set up a replacement schedule at installation.
Does a whole-home filtration system reduce water pressure?
A properly sized system shouldn't cause a noticeable pressure drop. We size the system to your household's flow rate specifically to avoid this.
