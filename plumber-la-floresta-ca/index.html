Plumbing Help For La Floresta's Mix Of Homes.

La Floresta is a master-planned community in Brea, built on the site of the former Unocal research and development center, which occupied the land for roughly 50 years. Plans called for about 1,400 homes, and other reports put the total near 1,357 residences.

The mix includes single-family homes, townhomes, live/work condominiums, apartments and active-adult residences. A call from La Floresta might come from a homeowner with a private water heater, a condo owner with shared walls and a building shutoff, or a renter whose landlord has to approve the repair. We ask which one applies before we dispatch, so the right person is involved from the start.

Local Housing.

Single-Family, Townhome And Condo Ownership.

The first single-family release, Avenida at La Floresta, opened in October 2013 with homes of about 2,400 to 2,675 square feet, up to five bedrooms and three baths, in Spanish Romantica, Monterey and Rancho Adobe styles. The community also includes townhomes, condominiums and apartments. Building age and ownership structure can differ from one address to the next, and that changes who needs to be involved in a repair.

What this means for pipe age: Homes at different La Floresta addresses may have been completed in different years, so we confirm what is installed at your address instead of assuming it from the neighborhood.

Before We Arrive.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In La Floresta.

Whether you own a house, a townhome or a condominium, the first few minutes matter. While you wait for us:

💧

Shut off the water.

In a house, find the main shutoff. In a townhome or condominium, find the unit shutoff, and if there is none, call your association or property manager about the building shutoff.

⚡

Protect people and belongings.

Move electronics and furniture away from standing water, and stay clear of outlets and panels near wet areas.

🏢

Tell your association or landlord.

If water may have reached a neighbor's unit or a shared wall, let your association or property manager know early so they can act too.
