Tankless water heaters need different maintenance than tank units, and a lot of the 'tankless problems' we get called for in Brea are actually descaling issues rather than unit failures. We service, descale, and repair tankless units from the major manufacturers.

Why This Happens In Brea.

Local Cause.

Orange County's moderately hard water means scale buildup inside a tankless unit's heat exchanger happens faster here than in a soft-water market, which is the single biggest factor in a tankless unit losing output or throwing error codes years before it should.

Homes in Country Road's HOA townhome community that have converted to tankless units are less common than in newer developments, so when we do get a tankless call there, we check whether the original gas line sizing actually supports the unit correctly.

Signs To Watch For.

Symptoms.

  • Fluctuating water temperature.
  • Error codes on the unit display.
  • Reduced flow rate at fixtures.
  • Unit not igniting.

How we diagnose it: We flush the heat exchanger with a descaling solution circulated through the unit's service valves, check the flow sensor and igniter, and read any stored error codes before assuming a part needs replacing.

Common Question.

Not Sure If It's a Tankless Problem or a Water Heater Problem?.

If your home has a tank-style water heater, temperature and flow issues are covered on our Water Heater Repair page instead, since the diagnostic approach is different. Tankless units fail differently than tank units and shouldn't be diagnosed with the same checklist.

What To Expect.

On The Visit.

We start by pulling any stored error codes from the unit's display and asking when it was last descaled, since that narrows the likely cause before we open anything up. Descaling is done through the unit's built-in service valves without disconnecting the water lines, and we retest hot water performance across multiple fixtures before calling the job done.

Timing.

Emergency Call or Schedule It?.

An error code that's stopped hot water entirely is worth a prompt call. Fluctuating temperature or reduced flow that's still usable, day to day, can typically be scheduled within the week for a descaling service rather than treated as an emergency. Either way, call (714) 750-8637 and we'll tell you honestly which one applies.

FAQs.

How often should a tankless unit be descaled in Brea?
About once a year given the local water hardness, more often if you notice temperature swings before then.
Why does my tankless unit run out of hot water with multiple fixtures on?
Tankless units have a maximum flow rate rather than a storage limit. If two showers and a dishwasher run at once, you may exceed the unit's rated capacity, which is a sizing issue rather than a malfunction.
Can I descale a tankless unit myself?
It's possible with a descaling kit and the right isolation valves, but most homeowners find it easier and more reliable to have it done professionally, especially the first time, since incorrect isolation valve use can damage the unit.

A tankless unit that's properly descaled on schedule will outlast a neglected one by years. If yours hasn't been serviced since install, that's usually the first thing worth checking.
