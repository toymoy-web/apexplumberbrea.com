A dripping faucet or a sink that won't drain properly is a common, usually inexpensive repair. We fix or replace kitchen and bathroom faucets, sinks, and their connected plumbing. We carry common replacement parts for most major faucet brands, which is why a straightforward cartridge or O-ring repair is usually resolved in one visit rather than requiring a special-order part. Most repairs are completed within the hour using parts we carry on our service vehicles.

Why This Happens In Brea.

Local Cause.

Original fixtures in Brea's older downtown-area homes and 1970s townhomes are common candidates for worn cartridges or valve seats, since faucet hardware from that era typically wasn't designed for the same lifespan as current fixtures.

Downtown Brea's mix of pre-1930s cottages and newer condos means fixture age varies block to block, so we don't assume a faucet's condition based on the neighborhood alone, we check the specific fixture's wear.

Signs To Watch For.

Symptoms.

  • Persistent dripping from the faucet spout.
  • low water pressure at one fixture.
  • water pooling under the sink.
  • handle is loose or hard to turn.
  • visible corrosion at the base.

How we diagnose it: We identify whether the issue is the cartridge, O-rings, or the valve seat, since a dripping faucet is almost always one of those three rather than requiring a full fixture replacement. We also check the supply line shutoff valves while we're under the sink, since a hard-to-turn or corroded shutoff is worth flagging even if it isn't the reason for today's call.

Options.

Repair Approaches.

🔧

Cartridge/O-ring replacement.

Fixes most dripping and hard-to-turn handle issues.

🔧

Full faucet replacement.

Makes sense for an older fixture with mineral buildup throughout or a style upgrade. We check for any related supply line wear while the fixture is open.

What To Expect.

On The Visit.

We identify whether the issue is the cartridge, O-rings, or valve seat before recommending a fix, since a full faucet replacement isn't always necessary for a drip. If you're replacing the fixture, we'll confirm the new faucet is compatible with your existing sink holes and supply line configuration before ordering or installing anything.

Timing.

Emergency Call or Schedule It?.

A dripping faucet is rarely urgent and can be scheduled at your convenience. Active leaking under the sink that's pooling water is worth addressing sooner to avoid cabinet or flooring damage. Either way, call (714) 750-8637 and we'll tell you honestly which one applies.

Answered First.

FAQs.

Why is my faucet dripping even when it's off?
Almost always a worn cartridge or O-ring not sealing fully anymore. It's a straightforward repair that also stops the water waste.
Can a dripping faucet raise my water bill?
Yes, a steady drip adds up to real gallons over a month. Fixing it usually pays for itself in a few billing cycles.
Can you match a specific faucet brand or finish I want?
Yes, we can source and install most standard faucet brands and finishes, or work with a fixture you've already purchased.

A dripping faucet rarely fixes itself and usually gets worse. It's one of the fastest, most affordable repairs we do. Even a small drip is worth fixing before the fixture itself wears out completely and a repair is no longer an option. We stock common parts on our service vehicles, which keeps most repairs to a single visit.
